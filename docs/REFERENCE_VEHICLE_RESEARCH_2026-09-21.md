# Reference vehicles and mission cases: research stage

Date: 2026-09-21. Repository baseline: `08e0e07`. No vehicle model, configuration, optimizer or simulator implementation changed. This report extends [the simulator audit](SIMULATOR_AUDIT_2026-09-21.md), not its physical validation claims. Repository migration/reproduction notes were read first; no applicable AGENTS.md was found.

## Recommendation and evidence labels

Use **HORUS-2B for a first published-model entry benchmark**, and **ARD for subsequent capsule flight comparison**. Retain **Apollo AS-202 / CM 011** as a capsule alternative if its missing mission-specific inputs can be obtained. For orbital work, start with an analytical circular-orbit relative-motion check, then an **HTV-2 operational-sequence benchmark**. These are separate cases, not a proposal to attach an ARD capsule to an HTV or to the current 2000 kg chaser.

Labels: **P** published numerical value/definition; **D** derived by an explicitly stated calculation; **G** graphical information requiring digitization and uncertainty; **M** missing or not resolved in the reviewed sources. P does not imply flight measurement: design, simulation, ground test and reconstructed flight are distinguished below. Missing means unavailable in this review, not necessarily nonexistent.

## Important source boundaries

**HORUS:** H1's original configuration and later powered Sänger variants must not be combined. H1 supplies clean/control-increment tables; H2 uses analytical fits and its own environmental/trajectory formulation. Selecting H1 data with H2 entry conditions is an explicitly assembled benchmark, not automatically exact reproduction of H2. Its Table 1 specifies qmax=10000 Pa, heating limit530 kW/m², load2.5 g and terminal altitude24.86 km/Mach2.5/distance0.75°. These are reference-case constraints, not measured performance or newly verified simulator results. The endpoint is before landing. Validate trim, out-of-grid behavior and the treatment of M>20 before any reference flight replay.

**ARD:** the user supplied both previously inaccessible full papers. A3 materially improves sufficiency: entry mass, coefficient area, geometric ratios and relative EI velocity/FPA are now published rather than assumptions. In addition to the formula, p.10-11 explicitly publishes Sref=6.1575 m²; the extra digits in the matrix are derived, not measurement precision. A1's preflight 7533 m/s and 3° entry must not be mixed with A3's postflight 7451.65 m/s and −2.6°. A3 itself has a timing discrepancy: p.10-4 says H0+1:19:06 (4746 s), whereas Table 1 says4750 s at120 km. Preserve both until the time basis is reconciled. Table 1 has DRS5281 s/14.1 km/M.6 and splash6074 s; p.10-11 gives deployment Mach.65. A2's event/accuracy values also differ (e.g. splash6079 s). Do not average revisions or treat rounded event rows as precise telemetry.

A3 Figs.14–15 and29–30 are useful trajectory-specific evidence, but not a full predictive database. It discusses atmosphere-reconstruction uncertainty and real-gas effects; fitting coefficients to its reconstructed flight and then using that same flight as independent validation would be circular. A3's AoA is signed negative in Fig.15, whereas comparisons also label 20° magnitude. Verify the axes explicitly. EI global state, coefficient coverage, control history and mass consumption remain dependencies. The report's internal references R1 (Rolland, flight control) and R5 (Paulat, aerodynamics) have been requested from the user; full texts have not yet been obtained. This is an explicit research gap, not a claim that their contents were reviewed.

**Johnston paper:** the uploaded filename says2012, but the paper is AIAA **2002-0407**, January2002. A4 reports 1:15.6 scale HEG experiments, α20°, zero yaw, two nominal conditions near Mach8.2 and8.1. Its velocities, densities and enthalpies are shock-tunnel conditions, not atmospheric EI conditions. Its geometry corroborates flight Rn=3.36 m; the model's radius must not be used as the flight heating radius. Surface heat-flux/pressure comparisons cannot directly validate a scalar Sutton–Graves stagnation surrogate or supply a CL/CD map. A3 additionally reports TPS inversion uncertainty and failed thermocouples: heating data are reconstructed with material assumptions.

**Apollo:** C1 is specifically AS-202/CM 011, not AS-201, Apollo11 or an interchangeable Block II capsule. Table II's evolving CG and Fig.1's configuration/ablator offsets matter for trim. Its reconstructed atmosphere and hybrid aerodynamic dataset are not equivalent to a standard-atmosphere forward simulation. Retain it as an alternative rather than fill its missing mass with an unrelated Apollo value.

### Accepted alternative: a simplified ARD surrogate

The user could not locate R1/R5 and explicitly allowed consideration of simplified aerodynamics. Those papers remain unavailable; no further access request is needed for this stage. A complete Mach/AoA table is therefore a dependency for general database reproduction, **not a prerequisite for every exploratory ARD study**.

Two future options are reasonable: (1) a declared constant/effective CD and L/D model for a bounded flight regime, or (2) a piecewise trajectory-specific force model digitized from A3 with its corresponding AoA history. Option 1 has lower implementation burden but requires a justified coefficient choice and sensitivity range; option 2 has more preprocessing burden and cannot predict arbitrary AoA manoeuvres. No coefficients or extrapolations are selected here. In either case use the published ARD mass/area/geometry, retain the `SURROGATE_ONLY` label, state the valid Mach/AoA domain, and keep uncertain bank/control history explicit. Do not adopt the current 60 kg capsule's CD=1.3/L/D=.25 merely because the code already supports it.

Acceptance: force conventions and coefficient provenance documented; bounded sensitivity results; timestep convergence; no claim of independent flight validation when the same flight curves set the coefficients. Reuse existing validated coefficient/dynamics components where their contracts fit, but do not confuse code reuse with validation of the new physical assumptions. This option does not authorize implementation in the current research stage.

## Verification and limitations of this stage

Read and cross-checked source definitions, selected tables and plotted domains; visually inspected H1 tables and A3 geometry/entry/force plots and A4 geometry/test conditions. Computed only transparent SI/geometry conversions. The accompanying source manifest records PDF hashes/page counts and access provenance. No curve digitization, simulation, test-suite rerun, trajectory optimization or model calibration was performed: this is documentation-only work, and the prior bounded audit baseline remains the relevant execution evidence.

The shortlist is deliberately small, not an exhaustive survey. Full source availability does not imply all requisite raw flight data are published. R1/R5 were requested; the user could not find either, and the simplified-model alternative above records that outcome. Apollo mass/normalization and all complete flight-state packages remain open. Source comparisons here establish feasibility and dependencies, not validated new simulator capabilities.
